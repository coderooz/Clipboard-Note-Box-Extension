## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: The extension is not showing up in my toolbar

**A**: Click the puzzle piece icon (Extensions) in Chrome's toolbar and pin Clipboard & Note Box.

### Q: PDF export is not working

**A**: PDF export requires the jsPDF library. Ensure it is properly bundled in the extension directory. See the [roadmap](README.md#roadmap) for planned improvements.

### Q: My notes disappear when I close the popup

**A**: Currently, notes are not persisted. This is a known limitation and is on our [roadmap](README.md#roadmap) for a future release.

### Q: Does this extension send my data anywhere?

**A**: No. All clipboard content and notes are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted to any server.
